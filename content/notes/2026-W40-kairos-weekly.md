# KAIROS Weekly 2026-W40

Week: 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-04. Research frozen 2026-10-05; latest verified trade date 2026-10-02.

## Executive Summary

ARK's W40 tape did not show a broad risk-on reset. It showed a preference for control points that already have scale or deployment receipts: four funds bought NVIDIA while four funds sold AMD on September 29; ARKK and ARKW added Cerebras on October 2; and ARKK bought BWXT one day after BWXT announced a $189 million naval nuclear fuel contract. At the same time, ARKG and ARKK added Intellia while reducing 10x Genomics, and ARKF's Airbnb/DoorDash additions were paired with a tiny Zillow trim.

The useful KAIROS question is therefore not “is ARK bullish?” It is whether ARK is narrowing capital toward infrastructure and operating bottlenecks while using liquid or less-proven exposures as the funding sleeve. The evidence supports selective control-point allocation, not a market-wide conclusion. The trade file is official but unreconciled and incomplete.

## Evidence By Issue: Top Three Kairos Moments

### 1. AI compute rotated toward scaled control points

**Fact — official trade file:** On September 29, ARKK, ARKQ, ARKW, and ARKX all bought NVDA. ARKK and ARKW also bought AVGO and CRWV. AMD was sold in all four of those funds; GOOGL was sold in ARKQ and ARKX. The repository contains 26 official rows for September 29, but the notification file does not establish motive, execution value, or complete net flow. [ARK trade notifications](https://etfs.ark-funds.com/hubfs/idt/trades/ARK_Trades.xls)

**Fact — operating anchor:** NVIDIA's latest reported quarter in the repository showed $89.0 billion of Data Center revenue, while CoreWeave reported approximately $104 billion of revenue backlog and 3.1 GW of contracted power. These are company-reported indicators, not proof that ARK's September 29 trades were caused by those figures.

**Inference / KAIROS view:** The capital map is a rotation inside AI infrastructure: toward scaled accelerators, networking, and AI-cloud capacity, funded partly by AMD and selected platform exposure. The non-consensus point is that the next edge may be portfolio-level control-point selection, not simply “AI up” or “AI down.” The falsifier is failure of AI-cloud backlog, capacity, or accelerator demand to convert into revenue and cash.

### 2. Cerebras and BWXT connected inference speed to mission-critical hardware

**Fact — official trade file:** On October 2, ARKK and ARKW bought CBRS, while ARKK bought BWXT. The two CBRS rows were 0.167% and 0.3032% of their respective ETFs; the BWXT row was 0.1218%. These are meaningful directional signals inside the funds, but not a full-portfolio allocation measure. [ARK trade notifications](https://etfs.ark-funds.com/hubfs/idt/trades/ARK_Trades.xls)

**Fact — official company receipts:** Cerebras reported Q2 core cloud revenue up 287% year over year to $127.7 million, more than 600 MW of data-center capacity live or under contract, and $25.4 billion of remaining performance obligations. BWXT announced a $189 million naval nuclear fuel contract on October 1 and scheduled Q3 results for November 2. [Cerebras Q2 results](https://investors.cerebras.ai/news-releases/news-release-details/cerebras-systems-fast-inference-cloud-business-nearly-quadruples) · [BWXT naval nuclear fuel contract](https://investors.bwxt.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bwxt-awarded-189-million-naval-nuclear-fuel-contract)

**Inference / KAIROS view:** ARK is keeping the physical stack beside the inference stack: fast compute needs capacity, power, and mission-critical manufacturing. The timing between the BWXT contract and the trade is a monitoring clue, not proof of causation. The next receipt is delivery, margin, and backlog conversion—not another announcement.

### 3. Genomics remained a selection trade, not a sector-wide endorsement

**Fact — official trade file:** NTLA was bought in ARKG and ARKK on September 29 and again in ARKG and ARKK on October 2. VCYT was bought on September 29. In the same window, ARKG sold TXG on September 29 and October 2, while ARKK sold TWST and TEM. The official repository therefore shows repeated selection inside genomics alongside funding sells, not a clean sector-wide buy.

**Fact — company anchor:** Intellia's September 8 announcement said the FDA accepted the BLA for lonvo-z with priority review and set a March 10, 2027 PDUFA date. Its October 2 release was an inducement-grant filing, not a new clinical efficacy result. [Intellia investor releases](https://ir.intelliatx.com/press-releases)

**Inference / KAIROS view:** The market transmission is milestone-sensitive healthcare data and gene-editing commercialization, but the current evidence does not prove approval, launch, or clinical success. The white space is a receipt ledger that separates regulatory acceptance, approval, launch, revenue, and repeat capital from one another.

## Capital Movement And Coverage

- **Weekly repository coverage:** 36 official notification rows from September 29 and October 2: 23 buys and 13 sells. The file contains no verified rows for September 28, 30, October 1, or October 3–4, so absence is a coverage gap, not proof of no trading.
- **Largest repeated buy cluster:** NVDA appeared in ARKK, ARKQ, ARKW, and ARKX on September 29. AVGO and CRWV appeared in ARKK and ARKW. NTLA appeared in ARKG and ARKK on both observed dates.
- **Funding / substitution:** AMD was sold across ARKK, ARKQ, ARKW, and ARKX on September 29. TWST, TXG, and TEM were reduced alongside the genomics additions; GOOGL and Z were also trimmed. These are funding or substitution observations, not proof of rejection.
- **October 2 breadth:** ARKF added ABNB and DASH, ARKW added AMZN, and ARKF trimmed a very small Z position. This keeps the tape mixed rather than turning it into a single-theme infrastructure trade.
- **Coverage limits:** ARK states that notifications are unreconciled, incomplete, and exclude some activity. The repository does not establish execution dollars, fund flows, full weekly net exposure, or ARK's motives.

The capital map therefore reads as selective control-point allocation: liquid AI infrastructure first, inference and nuclear hardware next, and milestone-sensitive genomics as a discriminating sleeve. The market-level conclusion remains unknown.

## Risk Analysis

- **Manager scope:** ARK is one manager; its tape cannot establish institutional consensus.
- **Causality:** The BWXT contract preceded the trade by one day, but public timing cannot prove that it caused the purchase.
- **Execution:** Cerebras must convert performance obligations and capacity into recognized revenue and acceptable margins. BWXT must convert contracts into delivery and cash generation. NVIDIA/CoreWeave demand must survive supply, power, and customer-concentration constraints.
- **Biotech:** FDA acceptance is not approval. NTLA remains exposed to regulatory, clinical, manufacturing, launch, and reimbursement risk.
- **Data:** The latest workbook is a rolling notification file with an October 3 `Last-Modified` date but trade rows through October 2. Missing daily rows and unreconciled values prevent a complete net-flow claim.

## Evidence and interpretation

ARK's official trade file shows a four-fund shift toward NVIDIA, alongside Broadcom and CoreWeave adds, partly funded by AMD sales. Smaller sidecars included ARKF buying Airbnb and Block while selling Zillow, ARKG/ARKK buying Intellia and Veracyte while reducing 10x Genomics, Twist, and Tempus, and ARKQ buying SpaceX while ARKQ/ARKX reduced Alphabet. The bounded inference is continued selective reallocation toward AI infrastructure and adjacent optionality, not a claim of a single ARK-wide regime change.

NVIDIA's official Q2 FY2027 release reported $89.0B of Data Center revenue, up 117% year over year, and said Vera Rubin systems were ramping at partners including CoreWeave. Broadcom's Q3 FY2026 release reported $16.7B of AI-semiconductor revenue, up 221% year over year, while CoreWeave's Q2 results reported approximately $104B of revenue backlog and approximately 3.1 GW of contracted power. These company facts validate the operating backdrop, but do not prove ARK's motive or the durability of the trades.
